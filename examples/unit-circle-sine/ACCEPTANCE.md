# Acceptance

- The unit-circle card contains a true origin-centered circle, not a semantic node-edge sketch.
- The x and y axes use equal scale, so the circle is not stretched into an ellipse.
- The point `P(cos θ, sin θ)` lies on the circle and is connected to the origin by a radius.
- A dashed vertical projection connects `P` to the x-axis and is labelled `sin θ`.
- A visible angle arc labelled `θ` starts on the positive x-axis.
- The sine plot appears to the right with landmarks at `0`, `π/2`, `π`, `3π/2`, and `2π`.
- A mapping connection makes the circle-to-wave relationship explicit.
