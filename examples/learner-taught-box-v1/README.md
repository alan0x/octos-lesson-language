# Learner-taught box surface area (3D)

Morgan (grade 6) teaches the surface area of a 4 × 3 × 2 box (decision 0008).
The lesson turns the 3D box with view intents rather than camera numbers:

- card 2 `view … show: "box#bottom-face"`: the box turns to show its bottom,
  seen from below and squared to the screen;
- card 3 `view … preset: "front"`: the front face, 4 × 2;
- card 4 `preset: "isometric"` while the total 2(4·3)+2(4·2)+2(3·2) = 52 is written.

A learner can still turn the box by hand; a later lesson view turns it again.
